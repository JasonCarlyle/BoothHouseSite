# Scheme 1 — Content Staging

Drop source material here before it gets processed into the site.

## Directories

- **drawings/** — Architectural drawings, floor plans, elevations (Avery Archive scans, PDFs, etc.)
- **photos/** — Any photographs of Scheme 1 renderings, models, or related imagery
- **documents/** — Correspondence, notes, catalogue entries, research documents

## What to upload

Anything related to Wright's original Scheme 1 design for the Booth House — the $125,000 estate
proposal that preceded the built Scheme 2. Useful material includes:

- Avery Archive drawings labeled "Scheme 1" from FLW Foundation Archives (FLW 1502)
- Perspective drawings or renderings of the unbuilt design
- Floor plans showing the more elaborate Scheme 1 layout
- Any correspondence referencing the original commission scope or budget
- Published reproductions from Storrer, Pfeiffer/Taschen, or Hitchcock

## How content gets processed

Once you upload material here, Claude Code will:
1. Extract/convert images as needed (PDFs → JPGs, HEIC → JPG, etc.)
2. Move processed files to `public/images/scheme-1/`
3. Update the Scheme 1 page at `/scheme-1` with the new content
