# References — BoothHouseSite

Internal reference document for all sources cited or used on the site.
Each entry carries the footnote number used in the site's inline citations.

## Footnoted References (inline citations on site)

**[1]** Storrer, William Allin. *The Architecture of Frank Lloyd Wright: A Complete Catalog* (4th ed.). University of Chicago Press, 2006. ISBN 978-0-226-77620-6. — Primary catalogue reference for all Ravine Bluffs structures, addresses, dates, and commission details.

**[2]** Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation Archives (FLW 1502). *Sherman M. Booth House, Glencoe, Illinois — Scheme 2: Study Images and Correspondence.* Avery Architectural & Fine Arts Library, Columbia University. — Source for design drawings, furniture specifications, commission correspondence, and the January 1910 Lusitania letter.

**[3]** Gill, Brendan. *Many Masks: A Life of Frank Lloyd Wright*. Putnam, New York, 1987. ISBN 978-0-399-13232-8. — Source for Wasmuth Portfolio context, the 1911 Mamah Borthwick scandal, and Booth's role as Wright's attorney.

**[4]** Pfeiffer, Bruce Brooks; Gössel, Peter (eds.). *Frank Lloyd Wright: Complete Works, Vol. 1, 1885–1916*. Taschen, 2009. ISBN 978-3-8365-0927-5. — Covers all Booth-period commissions; source for Prairie period design context.

**[5]** Nute, Kevin. *Frank Lloyd Wright and Japan: The Role of Traditional Japanese Art and Architecture in the Work of Frank Lloyd Wright*. Van Nostrand Reinhold / Routledge, 1993. ISBN 978-0-442-01419-0. — Source for Japanese influence on Wright's ornamental language during the Booth commission years.

**[6]** U.S. Department of the Interior, National Park Service. *National Register of Historic Places Registration: Ravine Bluffs Development* (NRHP Reference No. 78001137). Listed 1978. — Source for NRHP listing date and scope of the designation.

**[7]** "Biographical Sketch of Elizabeth K. Booth." *Alexander Street Documents.* documents.alexanderstreet.com/d/1009639930. — Source for Elizabeth Knox Booth's birth date, birthplace, education, marriage date, and children's birth years.

**[8]** Suffrage 2020 Illinois. "Elizabeth Knox Booth." suffrage2020illinois.org/elizabeth-knox-booth/. — Source for Glencoe Equal Suffrage Association founding (1912), index-card lobbying system, and June 26, 1913 bill signing.

**[9]** *Ableman v. Booth*, 62 U.S. 506 (1859). United States Supreme Court. — Primary legal source for the Joshua Glover case and Sherman Booth I's role in it.

**[10]** Cook County Recorder of Deeds. Warranty Deed, Elizabeth Booth to Julius and Elinor Abler, August 1958. Sale price: $38,500. — Source for sale date and price. Copy held in content-inbox/artifacts/Sherman Booth Documents/.

**[11]** Glencoe Historical Society. Sherman M. Booth portrait photograph. glencoehistory.org. — Source of the Sherman Booth portrait used in the Original Owners section.

**[12]** Hitchcock, Henry-Russell. *In the Nature of Materials: The Buildings of Frank Lloyd Wright, 1887–1941*. Duell, Sloan and Pearce, New York, 1942. — First comprehensive scholarly survey of Wright's work; establishes the critical framework for the Prairie period.

**[13]** U.S. Library of Congress, Historic American Engineering Record. *Ravine Bluffs Development Bridge* (HAER IL-14). loc.gov/item/il0461/. — Source for bridge documentation, measured drawings, and archival photographs.

**[14]** "Sherman Booth House." *Wikipedia.* en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherman_Booth_House. — General reference; cross-checked for addresses, dates, and ownership history.

**[15]** "Ravine Bluffs Development." *Wikipedia.* en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravine_Bluffs_Development. — Source for house roster, addresses, and development history.

**[16]** Heinz, Thomas A. *Frank Lloyd Wright Field Guide: Northern Illinois, Wisconsin, Minnesota*. Academy Editions, 1996. ISBN 978-1-85490-454-8. — Site-by-site guide to Glencoe and the Ravine Bluffs Development.

**[17]** Betsky, Aaron; Shapiro, Gideon Fink; Pielage, Andrew. *50 Lessons to Learn from Frank Lloyd Wright*. Rizzoli, New York, 2021. ISBN 978-0-8478-6536-9. — Photographs by Andrew Pielage from his October 2024 visit to 265 Sylvan Road (#500fllwproject).

**[18]** RISD Museum (Rhode Island School of Design). *Library Table* (accession no. 2004.28). risdmuseum.org/art-design/collection/library-table-200428. — Wright-designed library table from the Booth House commission, held in the RISD permanent collection.
